This guide walks you through everything you need to go from a brand-new account to a published, shareable article. By the end, you'll have a working workspace, a structured category, and your first piece of content live for your team.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create Your Account">
    Navigate to the dashboard and click **Sign Up**. Enter your name, email address, and a password, then check your inbox for a verification email. Click the confirmation link to activate your account and access your workspace for the first time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set Up Your Workspace">
    After verifying your email, you'll be prompted to name your workspace. Choose a name that reflects your team or company — this appears in your knowledge base URL and header. Once your workspace is created, go to **Settings > Team** and enter your teammates' email addresses to send them invitations.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a Category">
    Categories keep your articles organized and easy to browse. Go to **Settings > Categories**, click **New Category**, and give it a descriptive name (for example, *Getting Started* or *Product Updates*). You can nest categories to build a hierarchy that mirrors how your team thinks about your content.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write Your First Article">
    Click **New Article** in the left sidebar. Select the category you just created, then give your article a clear, descriptive title. Use the editor to write your content — switch between rich text and Markdown using the toolbar toggle. Add headings, lists, code blocks, and images as needed. Your draft saves automatically as you type.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish and Share">
    When your article is ready, click **Publish** in the top-right corner of the editor. Choose a visibility setting: select **Public** to make the article accessible to anyone with the link, or **Private** to restrict it to signed-in workspace members. Once published, copy the share link from the article header and send it to your team.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  For a more detailed walkthrough of account configuration, SSO setup, and workspace permissions, see the [Account Setup guide](/getting-started/account-setup).
</Note>
