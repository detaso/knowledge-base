Before reaching out to support, check here first. This page answers the questions our customers ask most often — covering everything from how categories and tags work to what happens when you delete your workspace. If you don't find what you need, our support team is always happy to help.

<Accordion title="What is the difference between categories and tags?">
  Categories and tags both help organize your content, but they serve different purposes.

  **Categories** create the primary structure of your knowledge base. They are hierarchical — you can nest subcategories under parent categories — and every article must belong to exactly one category. Think of categories as the chapters of a book.

  **Tags** are flat, flexible labels you can apply to any article regardless of its category. An article can have multiple tags, and the same tag can appear across many categories. Tags are ideal for cross-cutting topics like "billing," "troubleshooting," or "advanced" that don't map neatly to a single category.

  Use categories to define your navigation structure, and use tags to make content easier to filter and search.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How many articles can I have?">
  The number of articles you can publish depends on your plan:

  - **Free plan** — up to **50 articles**
  - **Pro plan** — **unlimited articles**
  - **Enterprise plan** — **unlimited articles**

  If you reach the 50-article limit on the Free plan, you will need to delete existing articles or upgrade to Pro before you can publish new ones. Draft articles count toward the limit on the Free plan.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I import articles from another tool?">
  Yes. You can import content from several popular sources:

  - **Notion** — connect your Notion workspace and select the pages you want to import
  - **Confluence** — export a Confluence space as an XML archive and upload it directly
  - **HTML import** — upload a ZIP file of HTML files to batch-import content from any tool that supports HTML export

  To start an import, go to **Settings > Import** and choose your source. Imported articles are created as drafts so you can review and edit them before publishing. Formatting is preserved where possible, but some rich-text elements may need minor cleanup after import.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How do I make my knowledge base private?">
  By default, new knowledge bases are public and accessible to anyone with the link. To make your knowledge base private — so only logged-in team members can view it — go to **Settings > Workspace > Visibility** and select **Private**.

  In private mode, all visitors are redirected to a login screen. You can still share specific articles with external users by generating a shareable link with a temporary access token from the article's share menu.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Does the knowledge base work on mobile?">
  Yes. The knowledge base is fully responsive and works on all modern mobile browsers. Readers and editors can access, search, and read articles on any screen size without needing a separate app.

  You can also add the knowledge base to your home screen on mobile Safari or Chrome for a faster, app-like experience directly from your browser.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I allow comments on articles?">
  Yes. Article comments let readers ask questions or leave feedback directly on an article. To enable comments, go to **Settings > Content** and toggle on **Allow article comments**.

  You can also control commenting on a per-article basis — open the article editor, click the **Settings** tab in the right sidebar, and toggle comments on or off for that specific article. Editors and admins can moderate and delete comments from the article view.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What happens if I delete my workspace?">
  Deleting your workspace permanently removes all articles, categories, members, settings, and associated data. This action cannot be undone after the grace period.

  When you initiate a workspace deletion, you have a **30-day grace period** during which you can cancel the deletion and restore your workspace in full. After 30 days, all data is permanently purged from our servers and cannot be recovered.

  To delete your workspace, go to **Settings > Workspace > Danger Zone > Delete Workspace**. You must be the workspace Owner to perform this action.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Is there a free plan?">
  Yes. The Free plan gives you everything you need to get started:

  - Up to **3 editors**
  - Up to **50 published articles**
  - **1 GB** of file and image storage
  - Public or private knowledge base mode
  - Full-text search
  - Custom branding (with "Powered by KB" badge)

  When you're ready to scale, the **Pro plan** removes article and editor limits, unlocks integrations, custom domains, SSO, and removes the branding badge.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I export my knowledge base?">
  Yes. You can export your entire knowledge base at any time. Go to **Settings > Export** and choose your preferred format:

  - **HTML** — a self-contained archive of all articles rendered as HTML pages
  - **PDF** — a single PDF document or individual PDFs per article
  - **Markdown archive** — a ZIP of all articles as `.md` files with front matter, ideal for migrating to another tool or storing in a Git repository

  Exports include all published articles and their metadata (title, category, tags, author, publish date). Draft articles are not included unless you select the **Include drafts** option before exporting.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How do I contact support?">
  You can reach the support team through two channels:

  - **Email** — send a message to [support@kb.app](mailto:support@kb.app). We respond to all emails within one business day (Pro and Enterprise customers receive priority responses).
  - **In-app chat** — click the chat bubble in the bottom-right corner of any page in your dashboard to start a live chat session during business hours.

  When contacting support, include your workspace URL and a description of what you were doing when the issue occurred — this helps us resolve your request faster.
</Accordion>
