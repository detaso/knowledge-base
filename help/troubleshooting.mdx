Most issues you encounter in your knowledge base have a straightforward fix. Work through the relevant section below to resolve the problem quickly. If you've followed the steps and the issue persists, the **Still Need Help?** section at the bottom explains how to reach the support team.

## Account & Access

<Accordion title="I can't log in to my account">
  Start with the most common causes:

  1. **Check your email address** — make sure you're using the same address your account was created with. Try searching your inbox for a welcome email from `no-reply@kb.app`.
  2. **Reset your password** — on the login screen, click **Forgot password?** and follow the email instructions to set a new one.
  3. **Check for SSO** — if your workspace uses Single Sign-On (SSO), you must log in through your identity provider (e.g., Google, Okta, Microsoft) rather than with a password. Contact your workspace admin if you're unsure.
  4. **Clear cookies and cache** — browser state can sometimes block login. Open an incognito window and try again.
  5. **Check your workspace URL** — each knowledge base has a unique subdomain. Confirm you're visiting the correct URL (e.g., `yourcompany.kb.app`).

  <Tip>
    If you see an "Account suspended" message, your workspace owner may have deactivated your account. Reach out to your workspace admin directly.
  </Tip>
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="I didn't receive my invitation email">
  Invitation emails are sent from `no-reply@kb.app` immediately after a workspace admin adds you as a member.

  1. **Check your spam or junk folder** — invitation emails are sometimes filtered by corporate mail systems.
  2. **Search your inbox** — search for "knowledge base" or "kb.app" in case the email was automatically sorted.
  3. **Ask the admin to resend** — workspace admins can resend invitations from **Settings > Team > Members** by clicking the **...** menu next to your name and selecting **Resend Invite**.
  4. **Check email filters** — if your company uses strict email filtering, ask your IT team to whitelist `@kb.app` as a sender.

  Invitation links expire after **72 hours**. If your link has expired, ask the admin to resend it.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="My SSO login is failing">
  SSO failures are usually caused by a misconfiguration between your identity provider and the knowledge base.

  1. **Verify the SSO settings** — a workspace admin should go to **Settings > Security > SSO** and confirm the Entity ID, ACS URL, and certificate are correct and match what's configured in your identity provider.
  2. **Check attribute mapping** — the identity provider must pass the user's email address in the SAML assertion. Confirm the email attribute is mapped correctly.
  3. **Confirm your account exists** — if Just-In-Time (JIT) provisioning is disabled, your account must be created manually before you can log in via SSO.
  4. **Check for certificate expiry** — if your identity provider's signing certificate has expired, update it in both your IdP and in **Settings > Security > SSO**.

  <Tip>
    Copy the error code shown on the SSO failure screen and include it when contacting support — it pinpoints exactly where in the SAML flow the failure occurred.
  </Tip>
</Accordion>

## Content Issues

<Accordion title="My article isn't showing up in search">
  If a published article doesn't appear in search results, work through the following checks:

  1. **Confirm the article is published** — draft articles are not indexed. Open the article and check that its status is **Published**, not Draft.
  2. **Wait for indexing** — newly published articles can take up to **5 minutes** to appear in search results. Wait a moment and try again.
  3. **Check visibility settings** — if your knowledge base is private, or the article is set to **Private**, it only appears in search for users who are logged in with the appropriate access.
  4. **Search for exact words from the title** — try searching for a unique word or phrase from the article title to rule out a relevance ranking issue.
  5. **Check the article's category** — if the category the article belongs to is hidden or unpublished, articles within it may also be excluded from search.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Changes I made aren't saving">
  The editor saves your work automatically every 30 seconds. If changes appear to be lost:

  1. **Check your internet connection** — the editor requires an active connection to sync changes. A brief disconnection can prevent a save. Look for a "Reconnecting…" notice in the editor toolbar.
  2. **Look for an unsaved changes indicator** — the editor toolbar shows a **Save** button and a "Unsaved changes" label when there are pending changes. Click **Save** manually.
  3. **Check version history** — go to **Article Settings > Version History** to see all saved versions. You may be able to restore a recent version that contains your changes.
  4. **Avoid multiple tabs** — editing the same article in two browser tabs simultaneously can cause one set of changes to overwrite the other. Always edit in a single tab.

  <Tip>
    If you're experiencing repeated saving issues, try a different browser or disable browser extensions — some extensions (especially ad blockers) interfere with WebSocket connections the editor relies on.
  </Tip>
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Images aren't loading in my articles">
  When images appear broken or fail to load:

  1. **Check the image source** — if you embedded an image by pasting an external URL rather than uploading it, that image depends on the external server. If the source URL has changed or gone offline, the image will break. Re-upload the image directly to the knowledge base instead.
  2. **Check your storage quota** — if your workspace has exceeded its storage limit, new uploads will fail. Go to **Settings > Workspace** to check your storage usage and upgrade if needed.
  3. **Try re-uploading** — delete the broken image from the article and upload it again using the image toolbar button.
  4. **Check for Content Security Policy (CSP) issues** — if you are embedding the knowledge base via the widget on a site with a strict CSP, the policy may be blocking image requests. Add `*.kb.app` to your CSP `img-src` directive.
</Accordion>

## Embed Widget

<Accordion title="The embed widget isn't appearing on my site">
  If the embeddable widget doesn't show up on your site after adding the snippet:

  1. **Verify the snippet is in the right place** — the embed script must be placed before the closing `</body>` tag in your HTML. Placing it in the `<head>` without the `defer` attribute can cause it to fail silently.
  2. **Check the workspace ID** — copy the embed snippet fresh from **Settings > Widget > Embed Code** to make sure you're using the correct `data-workspace-id` value.
  3. **Check for JavaScript errors** — open your browser's developer console (F12) and look for any errors related to `kb.app`. Script blockers or ad blockers may be preventing the widget from loading.
  4. **Check widget visibility settings** — in **Settings > Widget**, confirm the widget is set to **Enabled** and that the allowed domains list includes your site's domain.

  <Tip>
    Test the snippet on a plain HTML page first to rule out conflicts with your site's JavaScript framework or CSS.
  </Tip>
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="The widget shows no articles">
  If the widget loads but displays no content:

  1. **Confirm articles are published** — the widget only surfaces published articles. Log into your dashboard and verify you have published content.
  2. **Check your knowledge base visibility** — if your knowledge base is set to **Private**, the widget requires the visitor to be logged in. Consider switching relevant articles to public or using a token-based embed for authenticated users.
  3. **Check category filters** — if you have configured the widget to show only specific categories, make sure those categories contain published articles.
  4. **Clear the widget cache** — in **Settings > Widget**, click **Clear Cache** to force the widget to re-fetch the latest content.
</Accordion>

## Custom Domain

<Accordion title="My custom domain isn't working yet">
  After adding a custom domain in **Settings > Domain**, DNS changes can take time to propagate:

  1. **Wait for DNS propagation** — DNS changes typically take between **15 minutes and 48 hours** to propagate worldwide. Use a tool like [whatsmydns.net](https://www.whatsmydns.net) to check whether your CNAME record is visible globally yet.
  2. **Verify your DNS record** — confirm you added a `CNAME` record pointing your domain (e.g., `help.yourcompany.com`) to `custom.kb.app`. An `A` record will not work.
  3. **Check for conflicting records** — if there is already an `A` or `AAAA` record for the same subdomain, it may override the CNAME. Remove any conflicting records.
  4. **Confirm the domain in your dashboard** — go to **Settings > Domain** and make sure the domain shows a **Verified** status. If it shows **Pending**, the DNS check hasn't passed yet.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Visitors see an SSL error on my custom domain">
  SSL certificates are automatically provisioned once your CNAME record is verified. If visitors see an SSL error:

  1. **Wait a few minutes after verification** — certificate provisioning begins automatically after the domain is verified and usually completes within 5–10 minutes.
  2. **Force an SSL check** — in **Settings > Domain**, click **Re-check SSL** to trigger certificate provisioning manually if it appears stuck.
  3. **Check for CAA records** — some DNS setups include CAA (Certificate Authority Authorization) records that restrict which certificate authorities can issue certs for your domain. Add a CAA record that permits `letsencrypt.org` if you have restrictive CAA settings.
  4. **Avoid proxying through Cloudflare in "Full (strict)" mode** — if your domain is proxied through Cloudflare, set SSL/TLS mode to **Full** (not Full Strict) to avoid certificate chain errors.

  <Tip>
    If the padlock still doesn't appear after 30 minutes, contact support with your domain name — we can check the certificate provisioning status on our end.
  </Tip>
</Accordion>

## Still Need Help?

If you've worked through the steps above and the issue isn't resolved, the support team is ready to help.

- **Email** — write to [support@kb.app](mailto:support@kb.app) with a description of the problem, your workspace URL, and any error messages or screenshots. Pro and Enterprise customers receive priority responses.
- **In-app chat** — click the chat bubble in the bottom-right corner of your dashboard to start a live conversation with the support team during business hours.

When you get in touch, include as much context as possible — what you were doing, what you expected to happen, and what happened instead. This helps us diagnose and resolve your issue as quickly as possible.
