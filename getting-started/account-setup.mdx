Setting up your knowledge base takes just a few minutes. You'll create an account, configure a workspace that represents your team or company, and customize it with your branding before inviting your colleagues to collaborate. This guide walks you through each step from first sign-up to a fully configured workspace ready for content.

## Get Started

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign Up for an Account">
    Navigate to the sign-up page and create your account using one of the following methods:

    - **Email and password** — Enter your work email address, choose a strong password, and select **Create account**.
    - **Single sign-on (SSO)** — Select your identity provider (Google, Microsoft, or your organization's SSO) to authenticate without a separate password.

    <Note>
      If your organization has already set up an SSO connection, ask your admin to send you an invite link so your account is automatically associated with the correct workspace.
    </Note>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Verify Your Email Address">
    After signing up with email and password, check your inbox for a verification email from the knowledge base. Click the **Verify my email** button inside the message to confirm your address.

    - The verification link expires after **24 hours**. If it expires, return to the login page and request a new one.
    - Check your spam or junk folder if the email doesn't arrive within a few minutes.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Create Your Workspace">
    Once your email is verified, you'll be prompted to create your first workspace. A workspace is the shared environment where your team organizes and publishes content.

    Fill in the following fields:

    | Field | Description |
    |---|---|
    | **Workspace name** | Your company or team name, shown to all members and visitors. |
    | **URL slug** | The subdomain used in your knowledge base address, e.g., `acme.kb.example.com`. |

    <Tip>
      Choose a memorable, consistent URL slug — it becomes part of your public knowledge base address and appears in every article link you share. Short, lowercase slugs work best (for example, `acme` rather than `acme-company-2024`).
    </Tip>

    Select **Create workspace** to continue.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Customize Your Workspace Branding">
    Make your knowledge base recognizable to readers by adding your logo and brand colors. Go to **Settings → Branding** and configure the following:

    - **Logo** — Upload a PNG or SVG file (recommended size: 200 × 50 px) that appears in the top navigation of your knowledge base.
    - **Favicon** — Upload a square icon (32 × 32 px) displayed in browser tabs.
    - **Primary color** — Enter a hex value for buttons, links, and highlighted UI elements.
    - **Font** — Choose from the available typeface options to match your brand guidelines.

    Preview your changes in real time using the live preview panel on the right side of the Branding page, then select **Save changes**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Invite Your First Team Member">
    Bring your colleagues into the workspace so you can collaborate on content. Navigate to **Settings → Team** and select **Invite member**.

    Enter one or more email addresses, assign each person a role, and select **Send invites**:

    <CardGroup cols={3}>
      <Card title="Admin" icon="shield-halved">
        Full access to workspace settings, billing, and all content. Suitable for team leads and owners.
      </Card>
      <Card title="Editor" icon="pen-to-square">
        Can create, edit, and publish articles and categories. Cannot change workspace settings or billing.
      </Card>
      <Card title="Viewer" icon="eye">
        Read-only access to internal content. Useful for stakeholders who need visibility but won't write.
      </Card>
    </CardGroup>

    Invitees receive an email with a link to join the workspace. They must accept the invite before they appear as active members.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Workspace Settings

After initial setup, you can fine-tune your workspace configuration at any time. Go to **Settings → Workspace** to review and update the following options:

| Setting | Description |
|---|---|
| **Workspace name** | The display name shown to all members and in the browser tab. |
| **Workspace URL** | The subdomain or custom domain used to access your knowledge base. |
| **Timezone** | Sets the reference timezone for scheduled publishing and activity timestamps. |
| **Language** | The default language for the knowledge base interface and auto-generated UI strings. |

<Note>
  You can update any of these settings later by returning to **Settings → Workspace**. Changing your workspace URL will update all article links — notify your team and update any external bookmarks or integrations if you do so.
</Note>
