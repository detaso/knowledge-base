The knowledge base interface is organized into three main regions — the sidebar, the main content area, and the top navigation bar — so that everything you need is reachable in two clicks or fewer. Spending a few minutes learning the layout now will save you time every day as your content library grows. This page walks you through each area and the keyboard shortcuts that let you move through the product without lifting your hands from the keyboard.

---

## Dashboard Overview

When you log in, the **Dashboard** is the first screen you see. It gives you an at-a-glance view of your workspace and quick access to recent or important content.

The interface is divided into three persistent regions:

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Sidebar" icon="sidebar">
    The left-hand panel containing your full category and article tree, recent items, and favorites. Use it to browse and organize all content in your workspace.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Main Content Area" icon="file-lines">
    The central panel where articles, category pages, and settings screens are displayed and edited. This area changes depending on what you select in the sidebar.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Top Navigation Bar" icon="bars">
    The horizontal strip at the top of every page. Contains the global search field, notification bell, and your account avatar for profile and workspace settings.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## The Sidebar

The sidebar is your primary tool for browsing and managing the structure of your knowledge base.

**Categories and articles tree**

The top section of the sidebar displays your full content hierarchy. Top-level items are **categories** (folders of related articles), and nested beneath them are the **articles** they contain. Select any category to expand it and reveal its child articles. Drag and drop items to reorganize the hierarchy at any time.

**Recent articles**

Below the content tree, the **Recent** section lists the last five articles you opened. Use it to jump back to work in progress without searching.

**Favorites**

Star any article by hovering over it in the tree and selecting the star icon. Starred articles appear in the **Favorites** section at the top of the sidebar so your most-referenced content is always one click away.

<Info>
  Admins and Editors see the full content tree including unpublished drafts. Viewers only see published articles that they have permission to access.
</Info>

## The Editor

Select any article from the sidebar to open it in the editor. The editor fills the main content area and consists of three zones:

**Toolbar**

The toolbar runs along the top of the editor. It contains:
- **Formatting controls** — headings, bold, italic, inline code, blockquote, and lists.
- **Insert menu** — add images, videos, tables, code blocks, callout boxes, and embeds.
- **Publishing controls** — the **Save draft** and **Publish** buttons, plus the article status indicator (Draft, Review, Published).

**Writing canvas**

The large open area below the toolbar is where you write and edit content. Type anywhere on the canvas or use the `/` slash command to insert any block type without reaching for the toolbar.

**Preview mode**

Toggle **Preview** (top-right of the toolbar) to see exactly how the article will look to readers, including rendered components, table of contents, and custom branding. Toggle back to resume editing.

<Tip>
  Use the **command palette** (press `Cmd+K` on Mac or `Ctrl+K` on Windows/Linux) to instantly navigate to any article, category, or settings page. It's the fastest way to move around a large knowledge base without scrolling through the sidebar.
</Tip>

## Keyboard Shortcuts

Learning a handful of shortcuts eliminates most mouse trips and keeps you in the writing flow.

<Accordion title="Navigation shortcuts">
  | Shortcut | Action |
  |---|---|
  | `Cmd/Ctrl + K` | Open the command palette to search and navigate anywhere |
  | `Cmd/Ctrl + S` | Save the current article as a draft |
  | `Cmd/Ctrl + P` | Publish the current article (or submit for review) |
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Formatting shortcuts">
  | Shortcut | Action |
  |---|---|
  | `Cmd/Ctrl + B` | Toggle **bold** on selected text |
  | `Cmd/Ctrl + I` | Toggle *italic* on selected text |
  | `Cmd/Ctrl + E` | Toggle `inline code` on selected text |
  | `Cmd/Ctrl + Shift + 7` | Toggle ordered (numbered) list |
  | `Cmd/Ctrl + Shift + 8` | Toggle unordered (bullet) list |
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Editor shortcuts">
  | Shortcut | Action |
  |---|---|
  | `/` | Open the slash command menu to insert any block type |
  | `Cmd/Ctrl + Z` | Undo the last change |
  | `Cmd/Ctrl + Shift + Z` | Redo the last undone change |
  | `Cmd/Ctrl + \` | Toggle Preview mode on and off |
</Accordion>
