The Slack integration brings your knowledge base directly into your team's daily communication. Instead of switching between tools, your team can receive article updates in the channels they already monitor, search for answers with a quick slash command, and see rich article previews when someone shares a link — all without leaving Slack.

## What the Integration Does

Once connected, the Slack integration gives your team three core capabilities:

- **Article notifications** — Automatically post a message to a Slack channel of your choice whenever an article is published or updated in your knowledge base.
- **Slash command search** — Use the `/kb search [query]` command in any Slack channel or DM to search your knowledge base and get results instantly.
- **Link unfurling** — When a team member pastes a knowledge base article link into Slack, the integration automatically expands it into a rich preview showing the article title, description, and category.

## Connecting Slack

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Slack integration settings">
    Go to **Settings > Integrations > Slack** in your knowledge base dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start the connection">
    Click **Connect to Slack** to begin the authorization process.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authorize the app">
    You will be redirected to Slack's OAuth flow. Review the permissions the app requests, then click **Allow** to grant access. You must be a Slack workspace admin, or have permission to install apps, to complete this step.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your notification channel(s)">
    After authorization, select one or more Slack channels where article notifications should be posted. You can add or change channels at any time from the integration settings page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select notification triggers">
    Choose which events should trigger a notification — article **Published**, article **Updated**, or both. If you only want to surface brand-new content, select **Published** only.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save your settings">
    Click **Save**. Slack is now connected. You will see a confirmation message posted to your selected channel to confirm the integration is active.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Using /kb in Slack

Type `/kb search` followed by your query in any Slack channel or DM to search your knowledge base without leaving Slack. For example:

```
/kb search how to add a user
```

Results appear as an **ephemeral message** — a temporary message visible only to you, not to other members of the channel. Each result shows the article title and a short excerpt. Click the article title to open the full article in your browser.

<Note>
  The `/kb` slash command is available to all members of your Slack workspace once the integration is installed. However, search results always respect your knowledge base's visibility settings — private articles are only returned for users who have access to them.
</Note>

## Disconnecting Slack

To remove the Slack integration, go to **Settings > Integrations > Slack** and click **Disconnect**. This immediately stops all article notifications and removes the `/kb` slash command from your Slack workspace. Your articles and knowledge base content are not affected. If you want to reconnect later, follow the setup steps above again.
