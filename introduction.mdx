## Get Up and Running

Follow these three steps to go from a blank workspace to a knowledge base your team can actually use.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create Your Account">
    Sign up at the dashboard and verify your email address. Once confirmed, you'll land in your new workspace ready to start building.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write Your First Article">
    Click **New Article** in the sidebar, give it a title, and start writing using the rich text editor or Markdown. Save your draft at any time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share with Your Team">
    Publish your article and set its visibility to public or private. Invite your teammates to the workspace so they can read, contribute, and collaborate.
  </Step>
</Steps>
