The knowledge base embed widget lets you bring your help content directly into your product without asking users to navigate away to a separate site. A small JavaScript snippet is all it takes to add a floating help button to any webpage. When a visitor clicks it, a search-first overlay opens and surfaces relevant articles instantly — reducing support tickets and keeping users in the flow of your product.

## How the Widget Works

The widget adds a floating circular button to the corner of your page. When a user clicks the button, a full-screen overlay slides open with:

- **A search bar** at the top — the user's first interaction is typing a query, so the most relevant articles appear immediately
- **Category browsing** — users can explore top-level categories if they want to browse rather than search
- **Article view** — articles render inline with full formatting, links, and media, so users get answers without leaving your app
- **Prev / Next navigation** — if the article belongs to a collection, navigation controls appear so users can step through the full guide

The widget loads asynchronously and has no impact on your page's initial load performance.

## Embedding the Widget

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Settings > Embed">
    In your workspace sidebar, click **Settings**, then select **Embed**. This page contains your personal JavaScript snippet pre-filled with your workspace ID.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy the JavaScript snippet">
    Click **Copy Snippet** to copy the full embed code to your clipboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste it before </body> in your HTML">
    Open your site or app's HTML template and paste the snippet just before the closing `</body>` tag. This ensures the widget initializes after the page content has loaded.

    ```html
    <!-- paste before </body> -->
    <script src="https://cdn.kbwidget.io/widget.js" async></script>
    ```
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pass your workspace ID and customize options (optional)">
    After the script tag, call `KB.init()` with your configuration options. At minimum, pass your `workspaceId`. See the Configuration Options section below for the full list of available properties.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify the widget appears on your site">
    Load your page in a browser. A floating button appears in the corner you configured. Click it to confirm the widget opens and articles load correctly.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Configuration Options

Call `KB.init()` anywhere after the script tag loads. The `workspaceId` property is required; all other properties are optional.

```javascript
KB.init({
  workspaceId: 'your-workspace-id',
  primaryColor: '#ef7025',
  position: 'bottom-right', // 'bottom-left', 'bottom-right'
  defaultOpen: false,
  categories: ['onboarding', 'faq'], // filter to specific categories
  locale: 'en'
});
```

<Accordion title="Configuration property reference">

| Property | Type | Default | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| `workspaceId` | `string` | — | **Required.** Your unique workspace identifier, found on the Embed settings page. |
| `primaryColor` | `string` | `'#ef7025'` | Hex color code used for the widget button and interactive accents. |
| `position` | `string` | `'bottom-right'` | Corner where the floating button appears. Accepts `'bottom-right'` or `'bottom-left'`. |
| `defaultOpen` | `boolean` | `false` | Set to `true` to open the widget overlay automatically when the page loads. |
| `categories` | `string[]` | `[]` | An array of category slugs. When set, the widget only shows articles from those categories. |
| `locale` | `string` | `'en'` | Two-letter locale code for the widget UI language. Supported values: `en`, `fr`, `de`, `es`, `pt`, `ja`. |

</Accordion>

<Note>
  The embed widget respects your workspace visibility setting. If your workspace is set to **Private**, users must be signed in to your workspace to view articles in the widget — unauthenticated visitors see a sign-in prompt instead of content. Make sure your visibility setting matches your intended audience before deploying to production.
</Note>

<Tip>
  Use the `categories` option to tailor the widget's content to the page it's embedded on. For example, pass `categories: ['onboarding']` on your new-user welcome page, and `categories: ['billing', 'account']` on your account settings page. Users see only the articles most relevant to where they are in your product, which improves both self-service rates and article engagement.
</Tip>
