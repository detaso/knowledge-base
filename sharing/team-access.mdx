Your knowledge base becomes more powerful when your whole team contributes to it. You can invite colleagues to join your workspace, assign them a role that matches their level of responsibility, and give external collaborators scoped access without handing them the keys to everything. Every person you add helps keep content accurate, up to date, and owned by the people who know it best.

## Inviting Team Members

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Settings > Team">
    In the workspace sidebar, click **Settings**, then select **Team** from the left menu. You see a list of all current workspace members and any pending invitations.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Invite Member">
    Click the **Invite Member** button in the top-right corner of the Team page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the invitee's email address">
    Type the email address of the person you want to invite. You can invite multiple people at once by entering multiple addresses separated by commas.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a role">
    Select a role — **Admin**, **Editor**, or **Viewer** — from the role dropdown. See the Roles Overview section below for a description of each role.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send the invite">
    Click **Send Invite**. The invitee receives an email with a link to join your workspace. Pending invitations appear in the Team list until accepted.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To resend or revoke a pending invitation, click the **⋯** menu next to the pending entry in the Team list.

## Roles Overview

Each role controls what a workspace member can see and do:

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Admin" icon="shield">
    Full access to all content and settings. Admins can invite and remove members, change roles, manage billing, configure integrations, and publish or delete any article.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Editor" icon="pen">
    Can create, edit, and publish articles, manage categories and collections, and view all workspace content. Editors cannot access billing or workspace-level settings.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Viewer" icon="eye">
    Read-only access to all published content in the workspace. Viewers cannot create or edit articles. Use this role for stakeholders who need to stay informed without contributing.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

For a detailed breakdown of every permission each role carries, see the [Roles and Permissions](/admin/roles) reference page.

## Sharing a Link

To share a direct link to a specific article:

1. Open the article you want to share.
2. Click the **Share** button in the top-right toolbar.
3. Click **Copy Link** to copy the article's URL to your clipboard.

Send the link to anyone who should read it. Whether the recipient can actually view the article depends on your visibility settings — if your workspace is private, they must be a signed-in workspace member. If the article is set to Workspace-only, unauthenticated visitors see a login prompt even in a public workspace.

## Guest Access

On the **Pro plan**, you can invite external collaborators as guests. Guests can view specific categories without needing a full workspace account or a paid seat.

To add a guest:

1. Go to **Settings > Team**.
2. Click **Invite Guest**.
3. Enter the guest's email address.
4. Select the categories they should have access to.
5. Click **Send Invite**.

Guests receive a scoped invitation that only unlocks the categories you specified. They cannot see the workspace sidebar, settings, or any content outside their permitted categories.

<Tip>
  Use @mentions inside articles to loop teammates in on content that's relevant to them. Type **@** followed by a team member's name anywhere in the article body — they receive a notification with a direct link to the article. This is a great way to flag newly published content or request a review without leaving the editor.
</Tip>
