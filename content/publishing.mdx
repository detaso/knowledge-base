Every article in your knowledge base moves through a simple publishing workflow: it starts as a private draft, becomes visible to readers when published, and can be archived or unpublished at any time without deleting the underlying content. This workflow lets you write and review at your own pace, coordinate releases across your team, and retire outdated articles gracefully — all without risk of accidental deletion.

## Publishing an Article

When your article is ready for readers, publish it directly from the editor or from the article list view.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Publish menu">
    Click the **Publish** button in the top-right corner of the editor. A confirmation panel opens with visibility and audience options.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose visibility">
    Select who can see the published article:

    - **Public** — visible to anyone with the link, including anonymous visitors browsing your knowledge base.
    - **Workspace-only** — visible only to signed-in members of your workspace. Use this for internal documentation or content under review.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm and publish">
    Click **Confirm & Publish**. The article status changes from **Draft** to **Published** immediately, and the article appears in the knowledge base navigation under its assigned category.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Publishing an article does not lock it for editing. You can continue to edit a published article at any time — changes save as a new draft on top of the live version and only go live when you click **Update** (or when a scheduled update fires).
</Note>

## Scheduled Publishing

Schedule an article to go live automatically at a future date and time — useful for coordinating releases with product launches, announcements, or content calendars.

To schedule a publication:

1. Open the **Publish** menu from the editor.
2. Select **Schedule for later** instead of publishing immediately.
3. Choose a **date** and **time** using the date picker. All times are in your workspace's configured timezone (set in **Settings → General**).
4. Click **Schedule**. The article status changes to **Scheduled**, and a badge in the article list shows the planned publish time.

To cancel a scheduled publication before it fires, open the article, click the **Scheduled** status badge, and select **Cancel Schedule**. The article reverts to **Draft** status.

<Tip>
  Schedule major content updates to go live during low-traffic periods — typically late evenings or weekends for most B2B audiences. This limits reader exposure to any edge-case formatting issues and gives you time to spot and fix problems before peak traffic the next business day.
</Tip>

## Unpublishing

Unpublishing reverts a live article to **Draft** status. The article disappears from the knowledge base navigation and from search results, but all its content, version history, and metadata are preserved. You can re-publish it at any time.

To unpublish an article:

1. Open the article in the editor.
2. Click the **Published** status badge in the top bar to open the publishing panel.
3. Select **Revert to Draft**.
4. Confirm in the dialog that appears.

<Warning>
  Readers who have bookmarked or linked to the article's URL will see a 404 page after you unpublish it. If you need to keep the URL working while temporarily hiding the content, consider using **Workspace-only** visibility instead of unpublishing.
</Warning>

## Archiving

Archive articles that are no longer current but that you want to retain for reference. Archived articles are hidden from the knowledge base navigation and excluded from search results, but they are not deleted and remain fully accessible to editors and admins.

**To archive an article:**

1. Open the **⋯** (more options) menu on the article in the article list, or from the editor's top-right menu.
2. Select **Archive**.
3. The article moves to the **Archived** section at the bottom of the sidebar.

**To restore an archived article:**

1. Navigate to **Archived** in the sidebar.
2. Open the article you want to restore.
3. Click **Restore** in the editor's top bar.

The article returns to its previous category as a **Draft**. Review and update the content as needed before re-publishing.

<Info>
  Archiving is ideal for versioned documentation (for example, docs for a deprecated product version). It keeps historical content accessible to your team without cluttering the navigation experience for end readers.
</Info>

## Version History

Every time you save an article — manually or via auto-save — the editor creates a versioned snapshot. You can view, compare, and restore any previous version from the **History** panel.

**To open version history:**

1. Click the **History** icon (clock icon) in the right sidebar, or choose **View History** from the **⋯** editor menu.
2. The History panel lists all saved versions with a timestamp and the name of the team member who made the change.

**To restore a previous version:**

1. Click any version in the History panel to preview it. A read-only preview of that version loads in the main editor area.
2. Click **Restore this version** to make it the current draft.
3. The restored version becomes a new draft — the article is not automatically re-published. Review the restored content and publish when ready.

<Note>
  Version history is retained for **90 days** on the Free plan. On all paid plans, version history is retained **indefinitely**. If you need to access a version older than 90 days, upgrade your workspace plan from **Settings → Billing**.
</Note>
