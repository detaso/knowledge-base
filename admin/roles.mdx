Every member of your workspace is assigned one of three roles: **Admin**, **Editor**, or **Viewer**. Roles determine exactly what a person can see and do across the knowledge base — from reading articles to managing billing. Assigning the right role to each team member keeps your content organized, your settings protected, and your publishing workflow running smoothly.

<Note>
  **Viewer** seats are free and unlimited. Only **Editors** and **Admins** count toward your plan's paid seat limit. Invite as many stakeholders, reviewers, or read-only users as you need without worrying about cost.
</Note>

---

## Role Overview

The table below summarizes what each role can do across the key areas of your workspace.

| Permission | Admin | Editor | Viewer |
|---|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| View published articles | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| View draft articles | ✓ | ✓ | ✗ |
| Create and edit articles | ✓ | ✓ | ✗ |
| Publish and unpublish articles | ✓ | ✓ | ✗ |
| Manage categories and collections | ✓ | ✓ | ✗ |
| Manage team members | ✓ | ✗ | ✗ |
| Change member roles | ✓ | ✗ | ✗ |
| Access workspace settings | ✓ | ✗ | ✗ |
| Manage billing and plan | ✓ | ✗ | ✗ |
| Configure integrations | ✓ | ✗ | ✗ |
| Access the Danger Zone | ✓ | ✗ | ✗ |

---

## Admin

Admins have unrestricted access to every part of your workspace. This includes all content operations (creating, editing, publishing, and deleting articles and categories), as well as all administrative functions:

- **Team management** — invite members, change roles, and remove users
- **Workspace settings** — update the workspace name, URL, timezone, and content defaults
- **Branding** — configure logo, colors, custom CSS, and domain settings
- **Billing** — view and change your subscription plan, update payment methods, and download invoices
- **Integrations** — connect and configure Slack, SSO, and other third-party tools
- **Danger Zone** — permanently delete the workspace

Because Admins can access billing and irreversible actions like workspace deletion, assign this role sparingly. Most workspaces operate well with one or two Admins and a larger team of Editors.

---

## Editor

Editors are your primary content contributors. They can do everything needed to build and maintain a high-quality knowledge base:

- Create, edit, and delete articles
- Publish and unpublish articles
- Organize content into categories and collections
- View drafts and version history for all articles

Editors cannot access team management, workspace settings, billing, or integrations. This makes the Editor role the right default for writers, support agents, and anyone whose job is to create and maintain content.

---

## Viewer

Viewers have read-only access to your workspace. They can read any article that is visible to workspace members (including articles set to **Private**, which are hidden from public visitors), but they cannot create, edit, or publish anything.

The Viewer role is well suited for:

- Stakeholders who need to review content before it is published
- Executives or managers who want to stay informed without editing permissions
- External contractors who need temporary read access

Because Viewer seats are free and unlimited, you can add as many Viewers as your team needs without any plan restrictions.

---

## Changing Roles

Only **Admins** can change a member's role. To update someone's role, go to **Settings > Team**, find the member in the list, and click their current role badge to select a new one. The change takes effect immediately.

For step-by-step instructions, see [Invite and Manage Team Members](/admin/invite-users).

---

## Role Assignment During Invite

When you invite a new user, you choose their role at the time of invitation. The default role selection in the invite dialog is **Viewer** — change it before sending the invite if you need the new member to have Editor or Admin access right away.
