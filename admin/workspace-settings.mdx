Your workspace settings are the central control panel for how your knowledge base looks, behaves, and operates for every member of your team and every visitor. Changes you make here apply globally, so take a moment to configure each option carefully before you invite users or publish content.

## General Settings

Navigate to **Settings > Workspace > General** to update the following options.

**Workspace Name**

The workspace name appears in the header of your knowledge base and in browser tab titles. Use your company or product name so visitors immediately recognize where they are.

**Workspace URL / Slug**

Your workspace is accessible at `your-slug.kb.app` by default. The slug must be lowercase, contain only letters, numbers, and hyphens, and be unique across all workspaces.

<Warning>
  Changing your workspace URL will break any existing links that point to your old address. A redirect from the old URL to the new one is provided automatically for **30 days** after the change. After that period, the old URL becomes inactive. Update any embedded links, help center references, or external bookmarks as soon as possible.
</Warning>

**Description**

The workspace description is used as the meta description in search engine results. Write a concise, accurate summary (under 160 characters) of what your knowledge base covers to improve discoverability.

**Timezone**

Set the timezone that your workspace uses to display and evaluate timestamps. This setting affects when scheduled articles are published — for example, if you schedule an article for 9:00 AM, it publishes at 9:00 AM in the timezone you specify here.

**Default Language**

Choose the primary language for your knowledge base interface. This setting controls the language of system-generated UI elements (such as search placeholders and navigation labels) and signals the primary language to search engines via the HTML `lang` attribute.

## Content Settings

The **Content** section of your workspace settings lets you control article behavior and reader interactions across your entire knowledge base.

**Default Article Visibility**

Choose whether new articles are created as **Public** (visible to all visitors), **Private** (visible only to logged-in workspace members), or **Draft** (not published at all). You can override this setting on a per-article basis.

**Allow Comments on Articles**

Toggle comments on or off globally. When enabled, readers can leave comments at the bottom of any article. Individual articles can still have comments disabled independently. When you turn this off globally, existing comments are hidden but not deleted.

**Show Article Author and Last-Updated Date**

When enabled, each article displays the name of the author and the date it was last updated. This helps readers assess how current an article is and who to contact with questions. Disable this option if you prefer a clean, brand-neutral appearance.

**Enable Article Ratings**

Allow readers to rate articles using a thumbs-up / thumbs-down widget at the bottom of each page. Rating data appears in your analytics dashboard and helps you identify articles that may need improvement. You can enable or disable this setting at any time without affecting previously collected rating data.

## Danger Zone

<Warning>
  The actions in this section are **permanent and irreversible**. Proceed only if you are absolutely certain, and consider exporting your content first.
</Warning>

**Delete Workspace**

Deleting your workspace permanently removes all content, settings, members, integrations, and analytics data associated with it. This action cannot be undone, and our support team cannot recover a deleted workspace.

To delete your workspace:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Settings > Workspace > General">
    Scroll to the bottom of the page to find the **Danger Zone** section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Delete Workspace">
    A confirmation dialog appears asking you to type your workspace name exactly as it appears.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm deletion">
    Type your workspace name and click **I understand, delete this workspace**. The workspace and all associated data are immediately and permanently deleted.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If you are on a paid plan, canceling your subscription does not automatically delete your workspace. You must explicitly delete it using the steps above if you want all data removed.
