By default, your knowledge base is hosted at a `your-slug.kb.app` address. Connecting a custom domain — such as `help.yourcompany.com` — puts your knowledge base on your own branded URL, strengthens trust with your readers, and keeps all of your support resources under a single domain. Setup requires creating one DNS record with your domain registrar and then verifying it in your workspace settings.

<Warning>
  Custom domains are available on **Pro and Enterprise plans** only. If you are on the Free plan, upgrade before following the steps below.
</Warning>

---

## Setting Up a Custom Domain

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Settings > Custom Domain">
    In your workspace, navigate to **Settings > Custom Domain**. If you have not configured a domain before, you will see an empty state with an **Add Domain** button.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your desired domain">
    Type the full subdomain you want to use — for example, `help.yourcompany.com`. Apex domains (e.g., `yourcompany.com` without a subdomain) are not supported; use a subdomain such as `help.`, `docs.`, or `support.`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy the CNAME target value">
    After you enter your domain and click **Add**, the page displays a **CNAME target** value — a hostname like `custom.kb.app`. Copy this value; you will need it in the next step.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a CNAME record with your DNS provider">
    Log in to your domain registrar or DNS provider (such as Cloudflare, Route 53, GoDaddy, or Namecheap) and create a new **CNAME record** with the following values:

    | Field | Value |
    |---|---|
    | **Name / Host** | The subdomain you chose (e.g., `help`) |
    | **Type** | `CNAME` |
    | **Value / Target** | The CNAME target copied from the previous step |
    | **TTL** | 300 (or your provider's default) |

    Save the record. The exact steps vary by provider — consult your DNS provider's documentation if needed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Verify Domain">
    Return to **Settings > Custom Domain** and click **Verify Domain**. The platform checks for the CNAME record and confirms it resolves correctly.

    <Note>
      DNS propagation typically completes within **5 minutes to a few hours**, but can take up to **48 hours** in some cases depending on your DNS provider and TTL settings. If verification fails on the first attempt, wait a few minutes and try again.
    </Note>
  </Step>
  <Step title="SSL is provisioned automatically">
    Once your domain is verified, an SSL certificate is automatically requested and issued via Let's Encrypt. This usually completes within a few minutes. Your knowledge base will then be accessible at your custom domain over HTTPS.
  </Step>
</Steps>

---

## SSL / HTTPS

You do not need to purchase, upload, or renew SSL certificates manually. As soon as your CNAME record is verified, the platform provisions a certificate via **Let's Encrypt** automatically. Certificates are renewed automatically before expiry — there is no action required on your part to maintain HTTPS.

Your knowledge base enforces HTTPS at all times. Requests to the `http://` version of your custom domain are automatically redirected to `https://`.

---

## Redirecting Your Old URL

After you connect and verify a custom domain, all traffic to your original `your-slug.kb.app` URL is automatically redirected (HTTP 301) to your new custom domain. This preserves any existing links or bookmarks your users may have saved and avoids broken links after migration.

The redirect from `your-slug.kb.app` remains active as long as your custom domain is connected. If you remove the custom domain, the `your-slug.kb.app` URL becomes your primary address again.

---

## Removing a Custom Domain

To disconnect your custom domain, go to **Settings > Custom Domain**, click the menu next to your domain, and select **Remove Domain**. Your knowledge base immediately reverts to the default `your-slug.kb.app` address. Remember to delete the CNAME record from your DNS provider as well, since it will no longer serve any purpose.
