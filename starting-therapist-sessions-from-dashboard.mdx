This video explains how therapists can start a scheduled session directly from detaso’s scheduling dashboard. After setting an event in the dashboard for a specific time, the therapist can click the upcoming event and begin the session if it falls within the 15-minute unit. They can also schedule an appointment by selecting the location and treatment, saving it, and then accessing it from the dashboard at the scheduled time.
