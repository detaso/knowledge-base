Collections and categories serve different purposes. A category is a bucket that articles live in — it's about where content is stored. A collection is a curated playlist of articles — it's about how content is read. Articles in a collection can come from any category, and you control the exact sequence in which readers encounter them. This makes collections the right tool whenever you want to guide someone through a series of steps or concepts in a specific order.

## When to Use Collections

Collections shine in scenarios where the reading order matters or where you want to surface a coherent journey from scattered content. Common use cases include:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Multi-step tutorials" icon="list-ol">
    Walk readers through a process from start to finish, pulling in setup articles, concept explainers, and how-tos from across your knowledge base.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Onboarding checklists" icon="circle-check">
    Guide new users or team members through everything they need to read before they get started, regardless of which categories those articles belong to.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Product launch docs" icon="rocket">
    Bundle release notes, migration guides, new feature walkthroughs, and FAQs into a single, time-bound collection tied to a specific launch.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Defined reading orders" icon="book-open">
    Any topic where prerequisites matter — readers need article three to understand article seven — benefits from a collection that enforces the right sequence.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Creating a Collection

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Content > Collections">
    In your workspace sidebar, click **Content**, then select **Collections**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click New Collection">
    Click the **New Collection** button in the top-right corner. A blank collection editor opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a title and description">
    Give your collection a descriptive title and a short summary. The description appears on the collection's landing page and in search results, so make it clear what readers will learn or accomplish.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Search for and add articles in order">
    Use the **Add Articles** search box to find articles by title or keyword. Click an article to add it to the collection. Drag articles up or down using the handle icon (⠿) to set the reading order.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set visibility">
    Choose **Public** to make the collection visible to anyone who can access your knowledge base, or **Workspace-only** to restrict it to signed-in team members.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish the collection">
    Click **Publish** to make the collection live. Unpublished collections are visible only to workspace editors and admins.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Collection Navigation

When a reader opens any article that belongs to a collection, two navigation aids appear automatically:

- **Previous / Next buttons** — shown at the bottom of every article in the collection, letting readers step through the sequence without returning to the collection index.
- **Collection outline sidebar** — a collapsible panel on the right side of the article lists all articles in the collection. The current article is highlighted, giving readers a clear sense of where they are and what's ahead.

Readers who land on an article directly (for example, via search) see a banner at the top of the article indicating that it belongs to a collection, with a link to start from the beginning.

## Managing Collections

After a collection is published, you can update it at any time without unpublishing.

- **Add an article** — open the collection editor and use the **Add Articles** search box to find and append new articles. New articles are added to the end; drag them into position as needed.
- **Remove an article** — click the **×** icon next to any article in the collection list. Removing an article from a collection does not delete the article from your knowledge base.
- **Reorder articles** — drag any article to a new position using its handle icon. The updated order is reflected immediately for all readers.
- **Duplicate a collection** — open the collection's settings (gear icon) and click **Duplicate**. The duplicate is created as a draft with the same articles and order, ready for you to modify.

<Note>
  An article can belong to multiple collections at the same time. Updating the article's content updates it everywhere it appears — you never need to maintain separate copies.
</Note>
