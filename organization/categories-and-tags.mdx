Categories and tags are two complementary tools for organizing your knowledge base. Categories provide a hierarchical structure — think of them as folders that group related articles together. Tags work across that hierarchy, letting you label articles with shared themes no matter which category they live in. Used together, they give readers multiple intuitive paths to find the content they need.

## Creating a Category

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Settings > Categories">
    In your workspace sidebar, click **Settings**, then select **Categories** from the left menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click New Category">
    Select the **New Category** button in the top-right corner of the Categories page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter a name and optional description">
    Type a clear, concise name for your category. Add a description to help both contributors and readers understand what belongs there.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a parent category">
    To nest this category under an existing one, select a parent from the **Parent Category** dropdown. Leave this field empty to create a top-level category.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose an icon and color">
    Pick an icon from the icon library and assign a color to make your category visually distinct in the sidebar and on category listing pages.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Save">
    Click **Save** to create the category. It appears immediately in your workspace sidebar and is available to assign to articles.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Reordering Categories

Drag and drop categories directly in the sidebar to rearrange them in the order that makes most sense for your readers. Grab the handle icon (⠿) to the left of any category name, then drag it to its new position. Changes take effect immediately — all users see the updated order as soon as you release the drag.

To reorder subcategories within a parent, expand the parent category first and then drag the children into position.

## Editing and Deleting Categories

To edit a category, hover over it in the sidebar and click the **gear icon** (⚙) that appears to the right of the name. You can update the name, description, icon, color, and parent at any time.

To delete a category, open its settings and scroll to the **Danger Zone** section, then click **Delete Category**. Deleting a category does not delete its articles — all articles that belonged to the deleted category are automatically moved to **Uncategorized**, where you can reassign them individually.

<Warning>
  Deleting a category is permanent and cannot be undone. Make sure you reassign any important articles before or after deleting to keep your content organized.
</Warning>

## Creating and Managing Tags

Tags are created on the fly — there is no need to set them up in advance. To add a tag to an article, open the article in the editor and type in the **Tags** field in the right-hand metadata panel. Press **Enter** or **comma** after each tag to create it. If the tag already exists in your workspace, it appears as a suggestion as you type.

To manage all tags across your workspace, go to **Settings > Tags**. From there you can:

- **Rename** a tag — the change propagates to every article using it
- **Merge** two tags into one to consolidate duplicates
- **Assign a color** to a tag for visual identification
- **Delete** a tag — this removes it from all articles but does not delete the articles themselves

<Tip>
  Assign colors to related groups of tags to make them visually distinct at a glance. For example, color all product-area tags blue and all audience tags green. Readers scanning article metadata can instantly understand what a tag represents before they even click it.
</Tip>
