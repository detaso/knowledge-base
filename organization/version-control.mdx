Every time you save an article, your knowledge base automatically creates a version snapshot. This gives you a complete audit trail of how content has evolved — who changed what, and when. You can browse the full history of any article, compare any two versions side by side, and restore a previous version instantly if something goes wrong or if a recent edit needs to be rolled back.

## Viewing Version History

To open the version history for an article:

1. Open the article in the editor.
2. Click the **History** icon (🕐 clock) in the top-right toolbar.

The version history panel slides in from the right. Each entry in the list shows:

- **Timestamp** — the exact date and time the version was saved
- **Author** — the workspace member who made the change
- **Change summary** — a short auto-generated description of what changed, such as "Added 3 paragraphs" or "Title updated", plus any manual note the author added at save time

Click any version in the list to preview its full content in the main editor area without making it the active version.

## Comparing Versions

To see exactly what changed between two versions:

1. Open the version history panel.
2. Check the checkbox next to a version.
3. Check the checkbox next to a second version.

A **Compare** button appears at the top of the panel. Click it to open the diff view. The diff highlights:

- **Additions** in green — text that was added in the newer version
- **Deletions** in red — text that was removed from the older version
- **Unchanged text** in neutral — content that stayed the same

You can flip the comparison direction using the **Swap** button if you want to view the diff from oldest-to-newest or newest-to-oldest.

## Restoring a Version

To restore a previous version and make it the current draft:

1. Open the version history panel.
2. Click the version you want to restore.
3. Click the **Restore** button in the preview toolbar.

Before the restore takes place, your current draft is automatically saved as a new version — so you can always undo a restore by returning to the history panel and selecting the version that was current before you restored.

<Warning>
  Restoring a version replaces the current draft. The current draft is preserved as a version in history, but any unsaved changes you made since the last save will be lost. Save your work before restoring if needed.
</Warning>

## Version Retention

How far back your version history goes depends on your plan:

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Free" icon="circle">
    Version history is retained for **90 days**. Versions older than 90 days are automatically removed.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Pro" icon="star">
    Full version history is retained for the **lifetime of the article**. No versions are ever removed.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Enterprise" icon="building">
    Full version history with **audit log export** support. Download history as a CSV for compliance and review purposes.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Tip>
  When saving a significant revision — such as a major rewrite or a policy update — click **Save with note** instead of the regular **Save** button. Type a brief description of what you changed (for example, "Updated pricing section for Q3 changes"). That note appears alongside the timestamp and author in the version history panel, making it far easier to navigate history at a glance without needing to open each version.
</Tip>
